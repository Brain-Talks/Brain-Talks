Brain Talks

Welcome to Brain Talks! The place where neuroscience, filmmaking and STEM gather!
Brain Talks is an initiative for motivated students and aspiring future change makers.
Our competitions and events share our 2 main missions of: Fighting global medical-stigma and improving knowledge in brain science, STEM and medicine through science and art.
We are going to launch many competitions throughout the year, any student involved in science, art, filmmaking or math will find their competition!
Click down to see our open (or upcoming-soon) competitions, if you feel that there's a skill that you can use to make a change but can't find a suitable competition, please contact us! This is an initiative open to all aspiring students who love to have an impact on the future throught their talents!.


Why Participate in our competitions?
Brain Talks is a non-profit website, we host neuroscience competitions and filmmaking contests.
What participating mean for a student:
_Engage in advanced neuroscience and medicine concepts.
_Participate in raising awareness and fighting medical-stigma.
_This is a place for all talented students. Filmmakers, scriptwriters, artists, web_developers, essays lovers and students who are creative at research and advanced medicine,
everyone will find a competition that suits them.
_Top 1st winners from each country will be gathered to plan and launch their project, initiative or coduct their research. Develop your skills, and build a nice college profile!.
