International Medical Stigma Awareness Film Festival

Age eligibility: 15-19 years old


About The Competition:

Share your voice, share others' voices, share our voices. The International Medical Stigma Awareness Film Festival requires participants to create films sharing stories, values and holding a meaning. There are mainly two categories. Social Activisim Section, where you create movies with a creative story that has a positive impact, and aims at sharing awareness about medical stigma. The Scientific Section, where you submit a unique scientific explaination of one commonly mistaken medical situation that faces medical stigma.

Cost: Free
Film Duration:4-10 minutes
Format: Mp4
Language: English

Please note: Films in other languages must have English subtitle.

Rules:

• Use AI only for helping in animating some scenes.
• AI for story or content generation is strictly prohibited.
• One entry per category allowed.
• No hate speech, violence, or improper language.

Results:

• Top 10 International winners announced on website.
• Top 4 films published on the website.
• 1st National winners invited to a workshop for a Common International Initiative.
